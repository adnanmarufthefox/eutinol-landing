The Unhealed Wound Crisis

Chronic diabetic foot ulcers become trapped in the inflammation phase — incapable of progressing to healing. The consequences are severe, systemic, and largely preventable.

Why Do Wounds Stop Healing?

Chronic diabetic ulcers become locked in a persistent inflammatory state. Senescent cells accumulate, chemokine signalling is dysregulated, and the wound microenvironment turns hostile — blocking the transition to the proliferative and remodelling phases essential for closure.

Sustained inflammation blocks cellular repair signals

Senescent "zombie" cells accumulate and resist clearance

Standard dressings provide moisture — not bioactive intervention

Healthcare systems absorb enormous cost with minimal outcome improvement
